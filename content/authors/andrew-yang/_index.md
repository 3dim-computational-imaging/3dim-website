Andrew H. Yang is a first-year Ph.D. student at the Wyant College of Optical Sciences at the University of Arizona. He is currently participating in the SWI-NIH project as part of his OPTI 792 research rotation. His research interests include computational imaging, optical system design, and biomedical imaging. Andrew received his B.S. and M.S. degrees in Mechanical Engineering from Yonsei University, South Korea, completing his M.S. in 2024. During his master's studies, he conducted research on quantitative phase imaging (QPI) and participated in a Samsung-funded research project focused on developing computational imaging techniques for retinal imaging.
