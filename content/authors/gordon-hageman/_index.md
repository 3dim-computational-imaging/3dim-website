Gordon Hageman is a PhD student at the University of Arizona’s Wyant College of Optical Sciences, advised by David Brady and Florian Willomitzer. His research focuses on multi-camera computational imaging for coherent and incoherent applications, from Fourier ptychography and synthetic aperture systems to heterogeneous camera arrays and array telescopes. He received his BS in Physics and Astrophysics from Northern Arizona University.
