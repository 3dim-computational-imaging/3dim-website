Ameer Nessaee is a Ph.D. student at the Wyant College of Optical Sciences at the University of Arizona. He received his B.S. and M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Arizona in 2024 and 2025, respectively.
Prior to joining the 3DIM lab, he conducted research in biomedical optical imaging under Dr. Dongkyun Kang, working on deep learning applications in confocal microscopy. He also previously worked as a software engineer at Imagine Learning.
His research interests include computational imaging, biomedical optics, and machine learning.
